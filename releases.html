Version 17 · 2026-Oct-22:15:25 PT Latest

What's new

  • Today's date, automatically. Each new round now starts with today's date in Setup: when you open the app with no round under way, after Reset round, and after you save a round. No more old dates carried over from last week.
  • A round you've already started keeps its date, even if you finish after midnight.
  • If you tap ⛳ First tee shot hit while the date isn't today, the app asks "Round date is Tue Oct 20, not today. Use today instead?" with Use today and Keep date.

Guide

  • Setup Round section mentions the automatic date.

Version 16 · 2026-Oct-20:16:16 PT

What's new

  • Auto checkpoints (GPS), optional. Switch it on in Setup and the app uses your phone's location to ask Finished hole 4? Log it now as you reach the 5th, 10th and 14th tees and the 18th green. Tap Log or Not yet. It never logs by itself, and an Undo shows for a few seconds after logging. It's off unless you turn it on.
  • It only works while the Pace Card is on screen. When you come back to it after locking the phone, it asks about any checkpoint you've passed, with −2 min and −5 min buttons to set the real finish time.
  • A status line under the switch shows whether GPS is on and how far you are from the next spot. If location is turned off it says Location off, use the buttons. The Finished hole buttons work exactly as before.
  • Course maps for Fraserview, Langara and McCleery. Saved rounds include a new auto_gps count.

Guide

  • New "Auto checkpoints (optional)" section.

Version 15 · 2026-Oct-16:13:01 PT

What's new

  • Projected finish. The round clock in the pinned banner now also shows Projected finish ~2:53pm. That's your tee time plus the target, moved earlier if you were ahead at your last checkpoint or later if you were behind. When the round is complete it shows the time you Finished.
  • Quick notes. The 📝 Note panel now has one-tap notes: Waiting on group ahead, Lost ball search, Group behind waiting and Slow green. Each saves straight away with the highlighted hole. The hole buttons sit above them so you can pick the hole first. You can still type your own note.

Guide

  • Pace banner and Note sections describe the projected finish and the quick notes.

Version 13 · 2026-Oct-15:16:11 PT

What's new

  • 📝 Note button. After tee-off, a small 📝 Note button sits beside ⏳ Wait. Tap it, type a note, then tap Save note or the keyboard's ✓ (Cancel throws it away). Each note is stamped with the time and the stretch you're on (for example "holes 10-13 (next hole 13)"). Notes appear in the waits list, where you can edit or delete them.
  • Notes are sent with your waits. In the Wait Log CSV and the automatic upload, each note is a row whose comment starts with Note: (wait number "note", 0 minutes). Notes don't count towards total wait time or the number of waits.

Version 1 · 2026-Oct-13:19:10 PT

The first full release of the Pace Card, built and tested 2026-Oct-11 to 2026-Oct-13.

What the app does

  • Setup Round at the top of the screen: date, course, holes (18, Front 9 or Back 9), group name, target round time (type 410 for 4:10), scheduled tee time and your email (optional). The box stays yellow until course, target and tee time are filled in. "Done – collapse Setup" folds it away; it also folds by itself after hole 4.
  • Courses: Fraserview, Langara, McCleery, Kamloops Golf and Country Club, Burnaby Mountain and Riverway, picked from a drop-down.
  • ⛳ First tee shot hit records your actual tee time. Before you tap it, the button counts down to your scheduled tee time, turning yellow in the last 5 minutes and red once you're late.
  • Checkpoints after holes 4, 9, 13 and 18 (or 4 and 9 for a front nine, 13 and 18 for a back nine), with target times worked out from each hole's par and scaled to your target.
  • Pace banner pinned at the top: "X min AHEAD", "X min BEHIND" or "ON PACE", with a plain-language line such as "at hole 9 · round target 4:10". Green, yellow or red at a glance, plus a "Next" bubble showing the next checkpoint time.
  • Finished hole buttons that fold into a summary showing how that stretch went and the running total, with Edit and Undo.
  • ⏳ Wait button for timing waits on the group ahead, with quick comment picks; it becomes "■ Stop" with a live timer.
  • Round Complete panel after the last hole, with Save & Share Round CSV, Save only (email later) or Not yet.
  • Automatic upload: saved rounds go straight to the Drawmaster's Pace Log. Without an internet connection the app holds the round and sends it later. Emailing the CSV is now a backup.
  • History of saved rounds, with Share Rounds CSV and Share Wait Log CSV.
  • Interval allocation (optional, in Setup) to tune how the round's time is split across the four stretches, with a "How are these calculated?" explainer and sources.

Notable changes on 2026-Oct-13

  • Rounds are now sent to the Drawmaster automatically when saved.
  • Setup moved to the top of the screen, turns yellow until it's ready and has a Done button.
  • Course is now a drop-down. Added Kamloops Golf and Country Club, Burnaby Mountain and Riverway.
  • New Holes choice: 18, Front 9 or Back 9.
  • Clearer pace banner wording, plus a check that catches unlikely target times (for example 1:00 for 18 holes).
  • Tee-time countdown on the First tee shot button.
  • The wait button is now a smaller "⏳ Wait" button.
  • "Captain Email" renamed "Your Email" (Captain is reserved for the Club Captain).
  • Each saved round records the local date and time it was saved.
  • Interval allocation editing reworked: type any split, a running Total turns green at exactly 100%, and Save allocation only works at 100%. Cancel throws away your edits.

Fixes

  • Editing one Interval allocation % no longer changes the value you typed.

Guide

  • Guide published at wpgc-drawmaster.github.io/pace-card/guide.pdf, with install steps for iPhone and Android. Any browser works.
  • Covers automatic upload, Setup at the top, the new courses, the Holes option, the countdown, the ⏳ Wait button, the "Your Email" wording and the new "Tuning the Interval allocation (optional)" section.
  • "Internet connection" wording replaces "signal".
  • An automatic revision stamp appears on the title page and every footer.
